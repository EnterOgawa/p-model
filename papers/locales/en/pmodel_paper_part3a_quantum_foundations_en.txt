Abstract

This paper (Part III-A) explicitly separates what is fixed from first principles from what remains at the
level of an effective theory in the quantum foundations of the P-model. While retaining the dynamical
time-wave vector and the proper-time mapping fixed in Part I, it uniquely specifies the correspondence
between the wave function and the time wave by requiring consistency with the Noether energy flow,
and it organizes the minimum conditions under which the theory reduces to a Schrödinger-type form
in the short-wavelength, nonrelativistic limit.
For the Born rule, the phase-diffusion relation

to an effective derivation under four conditions: phase mixing, linear detection response,
weak backreaction, and frequentist coarse-graining. For measurement, the detector-side stable
state is taken as the reference, and the diagonal Lueders/Kraus update is recovered as a coarse-grained
limit of decoherence and conditional update.
Electromagnetism is treated with a two-layer conclusion. The transverse component of the time-wave
vector fixes the transverse-wave photon branch and the massless light mode, and it fixes the selection
equation that chooses a single fine-structure constant from a single frozen action. At the same time, the
origin of the local U(1) redundancy, charge quantization, and charge normalization remain outstanding
tasks on the already adopted effective-theory side. Within this organization, the source dynamics of
entanglement is fixed as three-wave mixing on the adopted U(1) sector. For quantum information,
the paper reaches a minimal bridge that connects phase relaxation time, gate fidelity, pair-correlation
retention depth, and the origin of error channels to existing results.
The point reached here is not to claim a replacement of quantum mechanics. Rather, it is to fix
the physical contributions for which the P-model takes responsibility as reproducible equations, de-
cision language, and audit procedures at the respective entry points of the Born rule, measurement,
correlation, and quantum information.

3 Discussion

This chapter crosses the theoretical sections 2.5-2.8 and organizes what has been fixed and what still

3.1 Quantum Foundations Fixed in This Paper

The point reached in Part III-A is not a declaration that quantum mechanics as a whole has been
replaced. Rather, it is that the foundational entry points for which the P-model takes responsibility
have been fixed explicitly. Specifically, this paper has fixed: (i) the minimum conditions under which
the phase of the time wave reduces to the Schrödinger / KG forms, (ii) the derivation of the Born rule
through phase diffusion and linear detection response, (iii) the coarse-grained Lueders/Kraus update
through decoherence and conditional update, (iv) the transverse photon branch and the selection rule
for the fine-structure constant, and (v) the nonseparable pair amplitude and the source dynamics of
three-wave mixing.
Electromagnetism, however, must be read on two layers. On the light side, the transverse photon
branch and the massless light mode are fixed, along with the selection rule that chooses a single fine-
structure constant from a single frozen action and its reference value. By contrast, the origin of local
U(1) redundancy, charge quantization, and charge normalization is still not closed from P alone, so
on the observational side the already adopted U(1) sector is retained.

The items remaining after the reorganization are not all unresolved in the same sense. First, for the

rule and the reference value as the canonical source, while local tables and auxiliary diagnostics are
left to the supporting surfaces of Part III-B / Part IV.
Second, the origin of local U(1) redundancy, charge quantization, and charge normalization remains
a truly unresolved item. The transverse photon branch, the massless light mode, and the selection
rule for the fine-structure constant are already fixed, but the full local source theorem including the
adopted U(1) sector is still not closed. Here Part III-A makes the missing theory-side piece explicit,
while Part III-B takes as canonical the verification surface using the already adopted sector.
Third, the weak interaction must be read with a separation between the theoretical mechanism and the
observational acceptance/rejection. On the theory side, the W/Z mass reference points on the same
family generated by the two-component coupled Q-ball have been fixed on family (17, 1, 1) under
the coupled-localization condition, with κcoupled,W = 1.6024037199, κcoupled,Z = 1.6817234304, and
relative reference-point errors = (2.40, 2.07) × 10−5 . Therefore the weak-interaction sector does not
remain without any footing at all. On the other hand, the paper’s acceptance/rejection judgment
takes the numerical audit of Part III-B as canonical, and at the present stage is fixed as adopt Route
A and reject Route B. Thus the V-A mechanism of Route B is retained as a theoretical candidate, but
not as an adopted operating mechanism.

4 Conclusion

The conclusions of this paper can be summarized in five points.

 1. The minimum conditions connecting the local phase of P and the proper-time mapping to the
    Schrödinger / KG forms have been fixed.

 2. For the Born rule, the detection probability p ∝ |ψ|2 has been effectively derived through phase
    diffusion and linear detection response under the four conditions of phase mixing, linear detection
    response, weak backreaction, and frequentist coarse-graining.

 3. For measurement, diagonal Lueders/Kraus update has been recovered in coarse- grained form
    from the detector’s stable basis, decoherence, and conditional update.

 4. For electromagnetism, the transverse photon branch and the selection rule for the fine-structure
    constant have been fixed, while the origin of local U(1) and charge normalization remain unre-
    solved.

 5. For entanglement, the nonseparable pair amplitude, the source dynamics of three-wave mixing,
    and the audit surface toward Bell datasets have been fixed, while the judgment itself is left to
    Part III-B.

For the weak interaction, the W/Z mass reference points from the two-component coupled Q-ball are
retained as theory-side references, while the canonical source for observational acceptance/rejection
is the numerical audit of Part III-B, and the present conclusion is fixed as adopt Route A and reject
Route B.

the final judgment table that decides theoretical acceptance or rejection is left to the verification
